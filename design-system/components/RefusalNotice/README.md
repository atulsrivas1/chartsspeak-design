# RefusalNotice

The honest "no": names the refusal family (Not stored, Stale, Basis mismatch, Not permitted), what was not done, and the way forward. The `--caution` variant covers "Proposed, not applied".

**Consumer provides:** a `cs-notice` with `role="status"`, the glyph, a `cs-notice__title` whose `cs-notice__kind` is one of the fixed family words, and a body with the next step (a utility command in `<code>` where one exists).

- Always say that nothing was calculated or drawn. Never draw a partial series behind a refusal.
- Use the four family words exactly. "Not permitted" is always followed by "nothing changed".
- In the rail it sits in the conversation. Over the chart, use the `ChartStateOverlay` instead.
