Spec
Lines
Two, 1px, chart-crosshair, dash 3 on 3 off. Identical to the engine's crosshair, so when the engine's point frame paints they coincide and nothing jumps.
Never on the overlay
No price, time, bar value, axis tag, tooltip, label, dot or "live" styling. No animation, no fade: shown or removed at once.
Values
Only the engine read-out (the OHLC row above the chart). It updates when the engine frame paints and always carries its own date, so it never claims a bar it isn't for.
Placement
Vertical snaps to the nearest engine bar_x (follows the pointer pixel in the reduced regime). Horizontal follows the pointer. Clipped to the pane's plot rect: never over the price or time axis.
Layer
Above the canvas, below every DOM card (state cards, Preview tag, popovers). pointer-events: none, aria-hidden.
Keyboard (A-G4)
Engine-driven, not the overlay. ← → step one bar (Shift: ten); near an edge the crosshair walks to the first or last stored bar. Home / End jump there. The live region says "First stored bar" or "Last stored bar" once, with the bar's date and stored values.
Phone
Hold 450 ms inside the 10 px slop: the chart gets the dashed hold outline and the lines follow the finger. Values stay in the read-out row, never in a bubble under the finger. Lift keeps the engine crosshair; tap clears.
Themes
chart-crosshair is #c5ced4 on dark (12.0:1 on chart-bg) and #3a444c on light (10.0:1).
