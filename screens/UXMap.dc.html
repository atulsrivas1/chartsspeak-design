The ChartsSpeak experience, end to end

Each lane is one journey, left to right. Each card names the step, what the person sees, the boards that draw it (grey) and the app stories that build it (outlined). A card with neither was built before this backlog. An amber "No story yet" means it is designed and agreed but nothing in the backlog builds it. Dashed cards are alternatives or later releases. Story coverage is in docs/STORY-COVERAGE.md; if a screen is not on this map, it should not exist.

By width

  • 1024 and up: menu floats over the chart, chat docks and pushes it.
  • 761 to 1023: both open as drawers over the chart, one at a time, icon-only pane buttons.
  • 760 and below: Menu, Chat and Chart tabs. Nothing folds.
  • Guaranteed from 320 to 1920 wide.

Always visible

  • Chart head: Menu and Chat buttons with badges, symbol, price, warnings, legend line, Options.
  • Chart foot: data badge, status line, render stamp in UTC.
  • A stale, delayed or partial warning is never behind a gesture.
  • Where a message goes: the line under the To chip.

Keys

  • [ menu · ] chat · f fold both
  • o options · Ctrl+K or / palette
  • Shift+drag or Shift+← → select · Enter ask
  • Shift+F10 context menu · Esc closes · 0 reset view

Promises

  • Stored data only, and every number says where it came from.
  • Agents propose; people approve. Undo is always one click.
  • Nothing leaves the machine without the sent-to line saying so.
  • Every hover has a click and a key. Text is 12px or larger.
