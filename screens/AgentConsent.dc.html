First-use consent and choosing who to ask

Consent is asked once per remote provider, in plain words, before anything leaves the machine. The To chip lists agents, then people; off agents show as unavailable.

What is not sentYour keys, other charts, other people's private messages, and files.

<Provider> is the company that receives the data (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral…). <Product> is what people call the agent (Claude, ChatGPT…). Local models never show a sent-to provider: they say Stays on this machine.
